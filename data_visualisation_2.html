    THE QUESTION AND THE METHOD

    What, who, why and how

    Domain and why

    Australian admitted hospital care. This page helps a general Australian audience compare how hospital activity has changed, how care differs by age and service, and how facilities are distributed.

    Who

    Residents and students who want an accessible overview of the national hospital system without needing clinical or statistical training.

    What

    Recent AIHW hospital separations, patient days and service groups, combined with ABS population estimates and Geoscience Australia hospital locations. Source table numbers and downloads are listed below.

    How

    Lines reveal change over time; maps compare place and facility concentration; the population pyramid compares separations by age and sex; area and flow diagrams summarise diagnosis mix and service pathways. Tooltips provide exact values without hiding the main story.

    DATA, METHODS AND ATTRIBUTION

    Sources and limits

    • AIHW admitted patient care — activity tables 2.1–2.9, including five-year separations, patient days and length of stay.
    • AIHW 2024–25 access tables — table 3.1 (all-hospital separations and patient days by age group and sex); used for the population pyramid.
    • AIHW 2024–25 services provided tables — table 5.1 (service flows) and table 5.3 (diagnostic groups).
    • Our World in Data, hospital beds per 1,000 people — 2021 country data, based on World Health Organization data via World Bank (2026), CC BY 4.0.
    • Natural Earth, Admin 0 countries, 1:110m — world-country geometry for the hospital-bed choropleth.
    • Geoscience Australia, National HealthDirect Health Facilities MapServer — 877 mapped hospital locations; © Commonwealth of Australia 2025, CC BY 4.0.
    • Australian state boundary GeoJSON — map outlines supplied by rowanhogan.
