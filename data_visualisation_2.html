THE QUESTION AND THE METHOD

What, who, why and how

Domain and why

Australian admitted hospital care. This page helps a general Australian audience compare how hospital activity has changed, how care differs by age and service, and how facilities are distributed.

Who

Residents and students who want an accessible overview of the national hospital system without needing clinical or statistical training.

What

Recent AIHW hospital separations, patient days and service groups, combined with ABS population estimates and Geoscience Australia hospital locations. Source table numbers and downloads are listed below.

How

Lines reveal change over time; maps compare place and facility concentration; the butterfly chart compares age and sex; area and flow diagrams summarise diagnosis mix and service pathways. Tooltips provide exact values without hiding the main story.
